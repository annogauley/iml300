How You Can Make an Impact

The Boundary Waters is a connected landscape of lakes, forests, wildlife, and communities. People can help protect its clean water by speaking up about mining proposals, supporting communities affected by fires, and following fire safety guidance.

Mining

Contact elected officials, share reliable information, and support efforts for lasting protection from sulfide-ore copper mining in the Boundary Waters watershed.

  • Take action with Save the Boundary Waters
  • Contact lawmakers with Friends of the Boundary Waters
  • Learn about the mining threat

Wildfires

Support people and local organizations affected by wildfires, check reliable fire and closure updates before visiting, and follow all fire restrictions and safety guidance.
